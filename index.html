The four foundations

Technology is only one piece. The real power comes from how all four pieces are designed to work together.

01

People

Users, leaders, analysts, and specialists bring context, judgment, and accountability.

02

Processes

Defined steps turn inputs into outcomes—everything from approving an invoice to serving a customer.

03

Data

Facts and observations are captured, organized, governed, and transformed into information.

04

Technology

Hardware, software, networks, and platforms provide the tools and infrastructure.

From signal to decision

Most information systems follow a simple rhythm: capture something, transform it, share it, and learn from the result.

1

Capture

Collect a transaction, observation, request, or measurement.

2

Process

Validate, calculate, classify, and store the input consistently.

3

Communicate

Make the right information available to the right person or system.

4

Improve

Use feedback and outcomes to refine the system and the work around it.

Everyday example

Ordering lunch online

A small order uses the full system: a person places it, a process routes it, data records the items and payment, and technology connects the kitchen, customer, and courier.
